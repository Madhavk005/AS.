About
Me

Hi, I’m Akshita Sharma—a visual storyteller who thrives on turning bold ideas into impactful designs.

With a strong foundation in branding, logo design, and social media strategy, I focus on crafting visuals that don't just look good—they communicate. Whether it's a dynamic logo, an engaging packaging concept, or a comprehensive brand system, my goal is always to create a memorable impact.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Visual Arts at JECRC University, I also serve as the Design Head, leading and mentoring young creatives. Additionally, I hold a Diploma in Graphic Visual Design from Arena Animation, Jaipur.

My services include logo design, brand identity, packaging, and social media graphics. I am dedicated to creating clean, meaningful visuals that perfectly align with your brand's unique story.

With hands-on experience leading creative teams, I transform abstract concepts into designs that resonate with audiences and deliver measurable results.
